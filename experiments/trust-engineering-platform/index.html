Trust Engineering Platform

Overview of an experimental platform for evaluating, verifying, governing, and operationalizing agentic systems.

Overview

The platform explores how trust can be shaped through architectural controls, evidence generation, and operating procedures.

Problem Statement

Enterprise teams need a practical way to assess whether an autonomous system is ready for business-critical responsibilities.

Architecture

The structure centers on observability, policy enforcement, evidence capture, and human oversight.

Experiment Details

Prototype workflows were tested in simulated decision environments to evaluate trustworthiness and control readiness.

Lessons Learned

Trust is best treated as a system property that requires both engineering and governance practices.

Future Work

Expand the control layer, strengthen evaluation traces, and connect the workflow to operational governance.
