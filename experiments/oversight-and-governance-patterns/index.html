Oversight and Governance Patterns

Practical approaches for human oversight, policy enforcement, and operational trust in agentic workflows.

Overview

The work highlights governance patterns that allow teams to retain oversight without sacrificing speed.

Problem Statement

Agentic systems introduce ambiguity and risk unless governance controls are designed into the workflow.

Architecture

The approach relies on structured policy boundaries, escalation paths, and operational review points.

Experiment Details

Patterns were explored in collaborative and decision-intensive scenarios to understand friction and value.

Lessons Learned

Governance is most effective when it becomes part of the architecture rather than an afterthought.

Future Work

Map these patterns into reusable enterprise playbooks and control frameworks.
