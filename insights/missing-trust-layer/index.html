Are We Missing the Trust Layer in Agentic Architectures?

A practical look at the architectural controls that should exist before autonomous systems act on business-critical decisions.

Overview

The insight raises a central question: are we building capability faster than we are designing the controls that make it safe?

Key Takeaway

Trust layers need to be treated as explicit architectural responsibilities, not incidental features.

Architecture Implication

Good architecture surfaces verification and accountability in ways that are visible to operators and stakeholders.

Application

These ideas help frame the design of enterprise-grade workflows and review systems.
