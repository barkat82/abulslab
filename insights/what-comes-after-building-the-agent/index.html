What Comes After Building the Agent?

Reflection on the validation, governance, and control work that follows initial development.

Overview

Building the agent is only the first step. The harder work lies in integration, control, and adoption.

Key Takeaway

Robust adoption depends on operating practices, evidence, review loops, and explicit guardrails.

Architecture Implication

Architecture must support change management, monitoring, and human oversight at scale.

Application

These lessons matter for teams moving from pilots into production deployment.
